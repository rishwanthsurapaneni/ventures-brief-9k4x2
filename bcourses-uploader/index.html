bCourses Uploader

A personal tool for one UC Berkeley student.

What it does

bCourses Uploader runs on the owner's own computer. It reads the owner's own course materials from UC Berkeley's bCourses (Canvas), such as assignment due dates, syllabi, announcements and course files, and saves a copy into a folder in the owner's own Google Drive. This lets the owner find their course materials in one place.

What it can access in Google Drive

The app asks for Google's drive.file permission only. That means it can see and change only the files and folders it created itself. It cannot read, change or delete any other file in the Google Drive account.

Who uses it

The app is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and it has no sign-up.
